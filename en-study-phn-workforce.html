Will Japan have enough public health nurses in the future?

What we found

Under current job-change assumptions, the model estimated a national shortage of 435–1,007 public health nurses in 2030.

How the study was conducted

Stock-flow simulation based on local government surveys, recruitment data and government statistics.

Why it matters

The findings can inform recruitment and return-to-practice strategies.

How to interpret the findings
These are scenario-based projections, not observed shortages.
