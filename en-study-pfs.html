Can community-based care prevention also reduce future care costs?

What we found

Participants had a lower care-risk score after the program. Their estimated six-year care benefit costs changed by ¥107,000 less per person than those of nonparticipants.

How the study was conducted

A six-month outcomes-based program in Higashiosaka; 192 matched participants and 192 nonparticipants, assessed one year after baseline.

Why it matters

Local governments can consider both health outcomes and estimated future costs in program evaluation.

How to interpret the findings
Costs were modeled, not observed. The groups were not randomized.

Key results

Care-risk score · participants11.9 → 11.5
Care-risk score · nonparticipants11.4 → 12.3
Difference in change in estimated six-year costs¥107,000 lower per person
