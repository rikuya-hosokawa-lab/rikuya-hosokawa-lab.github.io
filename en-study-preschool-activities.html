Are extracurricular activities related to preschool social skills?

What we found

Children taking part in sports, music or educational activities tended to have higher social skill scores.

How the study was conducted

Regression analysis of caregiver and teacher reports for 1,348 preschoolers.

Why it matters

Consider equitable access to diverse experiences.

How to interpret the findings
Observational findings do not prove a causal benefit of lessons.

Key finding

Children taking part in sports, music or educational activities tended to have higher social skill scores.
