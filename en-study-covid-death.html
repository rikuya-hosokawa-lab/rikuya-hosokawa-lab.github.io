Did the pandemic change where people died?

What we found

The proportion of hospital deaths fell in 2020 in every age group aged 40 and over. No significant level change in age-standardized mortality was detected by cause and age group at the pandemic onset.

How the study was conducted

National vital statistics, 2016–2023; interrupted time-series analysis through 2022.

Why it matters

The findings can inform planning for end-of-life care during public health emergencies.

How to interpret the findings
The observational time series cannot isolate the pandemic as the sole cause.

Finding at a glance
