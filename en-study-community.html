Can community centers support older adults' social participation?

What we found

Regular users were more likely to increase participation in sports and volunteering and maintain everyday functional abilities.

How the study was conducted

A small community study of 72 respondents, comparing regular and nonregular users with statistical weighting.

Why it matters

Accessible gathering places may support independent living and social participation.

How to interpret the findings
Small observational sample; estimates are uncertain and may not generalize.

Finding at a glance

Regular users were more likely to increase participation in sports and volunteering and maintain everyday functional abilities.
