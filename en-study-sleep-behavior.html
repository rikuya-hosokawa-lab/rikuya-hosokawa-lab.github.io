Are sleep problems related to behavioral difficulties in adolescents?

What we found

Poor sleep quality, sleep difficulties, daytime arousal difficulties and sleep disturbances were associated with more behavioral problems.

How the study was conducted

Propensity-weighted analysis of parent reports for first-year junior high school students.

Why it matters

Consider sleep quality as well as duration in adolescent support.

How to interpret the findings
Cross-sectional data cannot establish direction of causality.

Key finding

Poor sleep quality, sleep difficulties, daytime arousal difficulties and sleep disturbances were associated with more behavioral problems.

Full text publicly accessible. Illustration is conceptual, not study data.

Original article / source：Hosokawa R, Tomozawa R, Fujimoto M, Anzai S, Sato M, Tazoe H, Katsura T. Association between sleep habits and behavioral problems in early adolescence: a descriptive study. BMC Psychology. 2022; 10(1):254.
