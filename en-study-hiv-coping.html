How is stress coping related to health among men living with HIV?

What we found

Stronger sense of coherence was associated with fewer anxiety and depression symptoms, but not significantly with adherence, CD4 count or viral load.

How the study was conducted

Cross-sectional HIV Futures Japan survey of 870 men under 65.

Why it matters

Psychosocial support can complement clinical care.

How to interpret the findings
Self-reported cross-sectional data cannot establish causality.

Key finding

Stronger sense of coherence was associated with fewer anxiety and depression symptoms, but not significantly with adherence, CD4 count or viral load.

Full text publicly accessible. Illustration is conceptual, not study data.

Original article / source：Togari T, Inoue Y, Abe S, Hosokawa R, Takaku Y. HIV-Related Health Status, Adherence, and Stress Coping Capacity among Men Living with HIV in Japan. HIV/AIDS Research and Treatment - Open Journal. 2018; 5(1):1-8.
