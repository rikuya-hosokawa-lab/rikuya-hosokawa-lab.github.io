Are long-term care insurer evaluation scores related to healthy life expectancy trends?

What we found

Municipalities with higher scores for maintaining or improving care needs tended to show larger healthy life expectancy gains and smaller changes in unhealthy years.

How the study was conducted

Analysis of 1,147 municipalities, 2017–2021.

Why it matters

Informs ongoing evaluation of long-term care prevention.

How to interpret the findings
Ecological study summarized from the public abstract; individual effects are unknown.

Key finding

Municipalities with higher scores for maintaining or improving care needs tended to show larger healthy life expectancy gains and smaller changes in unhealthy years.
