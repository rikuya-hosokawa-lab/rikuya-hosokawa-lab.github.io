What is related to parenting stress in mothers of children with eczema?

What we found

Lower family cohesion and flexibility and greater social burden were associated with higher parenting stress. Mothers working full-time tended to report lower parenting stress.

How the study was conducted

Regression analysis of mothers of children aged 2–6 with atopic dermatitis.

Why it matters

Family circumstances matter alongside dermatological care.

How to interpret the findings
Observational findings summarized from the public abstract.

Key finding

Lower family cohesion and flexibility and greater social burden were associated with higher parenting stress. Mothers working full-time tended to report lower parenting stress.
