Can family-planning education for men help?

What we found

Some studies showed improvements in knowledge, attitudes, practices or gender dynamics. Evidence certainty was low or very low.

How the study was conducted

Systematic review of five randomized or cluster-randomized trials in low- and middle-income countries.

Why it matters

Context-appropriate education that includes men may be worth evaluating.

How to interpret the findings
Heterogeneity, bias and imprecision limit conclusions about effectiveness.

Finding at a glance

Some studies showed improvements in knowledge, attitudes, practices or gender dynamics. Evidence certainty was low or very low.
