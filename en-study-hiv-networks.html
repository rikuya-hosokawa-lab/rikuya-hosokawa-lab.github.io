What kinds of social connections relate to mental health among people living with HIV?

What we found

Men living with HIV who had fewer face-to-face supporters tended to report more depression and anxiety. No clear direct association was found for the number of online supporters.

How the study was conducted

Cross-sectional HIV Futures Japan online survey (2013–2014), 879 respondents.

Why it matters

Accessible and trusted in-person support can complement other services.

How to interpret the findings
This historical survey does not imply that online support is ineffective or establish causation.

Finding at a glance

Men living with HIV who had fewer face-to-face supporters tended to report more depression and anxiety. No clear direct association was found for the number of online supporters.
