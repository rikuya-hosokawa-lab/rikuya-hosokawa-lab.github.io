Are attitudes toward older people related to one's own mental health?

What we found

Middle-aged adults who were more avoidant of older people tended to view their own aging more negatively and report more psychological distress and physical symptoms.

How the study was conducted

Cross-sectional path analysis of 1,213 Japanese adults aged 40–59.

Why it matters

Reducing age-based prejudice and supporting balanced views of aging may be relevant to well-being.

How to interpret the findings
The study does not establish causal pathways or diagnose illness.

Finding at a glance

Middle-aged adults who were more avoidant of older people tended to view their own aging more negatively and report more psychological distress and physical symptoms.
