Do regular community center users go out and talk with others more often?

What we found

Weekly users were more likely than less frequent users to report increased opportunities for outings and conversations.

How the study was conducted

Comparison of changes among 95 older residents in Tokai City.

Why it matters

Local gathering places may support participation.

How to interpret the findings
Small observational study; causal effects remain uncertain.

Key finding

Weekly users were more likely than less frequent users to report increased opportunities for outings and conversations.
