Do communities with greater social participation have longer healthy life expectancy?

What we found

Among men, municipalities with more hobby, sports and volunteer participation, outings and dental visits tended to have longer healthy and total life expectancy.

How the study was conducted

Ecological analysis of 85 JAGES municipalities.

Why it matters

Supports consideration of participation and access to dental care.

How to interpret the findings
Area-level associations do not establish individual effects.

Key finding

Among men, municipalities with more hobby, sports and volunteer participation, outings and dental visits tended to have longer healthy and total life expectancy.
