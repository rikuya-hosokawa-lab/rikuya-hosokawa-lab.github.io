Can mortality and disability data estimate healthy life expectancy?

What we found

Age-adjusted mortality and disability prevalence predicted regional health expectancy closely: adjusted R² was 0.968 for men and 0.994 for women.

How the study was conducted

Regression models using population and long-term care data from 344 medical areas.

Why it matters

These measures may help monitor regional health promotion goals.

How to interpret the findings
Model fit in regional data is not individual or external predictive accuracy.

Key results

Men · adjusted R²0.968
Women · adjusted R²0.994
