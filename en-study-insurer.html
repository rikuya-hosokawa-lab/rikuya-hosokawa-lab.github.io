Which municipal health programs are linked to longer healthy lives?

What we found

Municipalities with higher scores for health checks, health guidance and community-based integrated care tended to show greater gains in healthy life expectancy.

How the study was conducted

Analysis of 1,147 municipalities, considering baseline healthy life expectancy, income and population density.

Why it matters

The results can help municipalities evaluate priorities for health programs.

How to interpret the findings
Municipality-level associations do not establish program effectiveness.

Finding at a glance

Municipalities with higher scores for health checks, health guidance and community-based integrated care tended to show greater gains in healthy life expectancy.
