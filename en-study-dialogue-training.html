Can dialogue-based training support child guidance staff?

What we found

Training deepened participants’ understanding and highlighted challenges of dialogue between families and authorities and the value of early contact.

How the study was conducted

Exploratory evaluation including an interview with a staff member.

Why it matters

Findings inform training for collaborative family support.

How to interpret the findings
This does not demonstrate an effect on child abuse incidence.

Key finding

Training deepened participants’ understanding and highlighted challenges of dialogue between families and authorities and the value of early contact.

Full text publicly accessible. Illustration is conceptual, not study data.
