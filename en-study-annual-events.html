Do shared seasonal celebrations relate to children's social behavior?

What we found

Children who experienced more family events tended to show more helpful and considerate behavior, even after adjustment for family background.

How the study was conducted

Cross-sectional questionnaire study of 653 fifth graders in 2019.

Why it matters

Shared cultural and personal experiences may provide opportunities for social development.

How to interpret the findings
This observational study does not show that adding events causes improvement.

Finding at a glance

Children who experienced more family events tended to show more helpful and considerate behavior, even after adjustment for family background.
