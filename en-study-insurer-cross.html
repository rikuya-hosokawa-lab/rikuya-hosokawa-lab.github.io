Do municipalities with higher program evaluation scores have longer healthy life expectancy?

What we found

Higher scores for health checks and guidance, duplicate/polypharmacy initiatives and integrated community care were associated with longer healthy life expectancy.

How the study was conducted

Ecological comparison of 1,154 municipalities in 2020.

Why it matters

Informs evaluation of local prevention programs.

How to interpret the findings
Cross-sectional ecological evidence; public abstract summary.

Key finding

Higher scores for health checks and guidance, duplicate/polypharmacy initiatives and integrated community care were associated with longer healthy life expectancy.
