Do municipalities delivering more health programs see larger gains in healthy life expectancy?

What we found

Municipalities providing more specific health guidance tended to have greater gains in healthy life expectancy for both sexes. Similar patterns were observed for specific health checkups in men and community care-prevention support in women.

How the study was conducted

Ecological analysis of program delivery and changes in healthy life expectancy.

Why it matters

Evaluate quality and implementation as well as volume.

How to interpret the findings
Public abstract summary; ecological associations are not causal effects.

Key finding
