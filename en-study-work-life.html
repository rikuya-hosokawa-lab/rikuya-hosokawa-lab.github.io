How does work–life balance relate to children's mental health?

What we found

When mothers reported more negative spillover between work and family, children tended to have more emotional and behavioral problems; positive spillover was linked to fewer problems and more prosocial behavior.

How the study was conducted

Parent questionnaire data for fifth graders in Aichi in 2019, analyzed with regression and path models.

Why it matters

Workplace and family support may matter for children's well-being as well as parents'.

How to interpret the findings
Cross-sectional associations and modeled pathways do not demonstrate causal effects.

Finding at a glance
