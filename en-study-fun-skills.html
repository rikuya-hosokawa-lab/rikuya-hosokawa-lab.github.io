Can Fun FRIENDS help preschoolers develop emotional and social skills?

What we found

Children who took part improved in self-control and cooperation; both scores were higher than in the comparison group after the program.

How the study was conducted

Ten weekly classroom sessions for 4–5-year-olds; 94 intervention and 66 comparison children analyzed.

Why it matters

Preschool can provide opportunities to learn emotion regulation and cooperation.

How to interpret the findings
Nonrandomized intervention; this cohort also appears in the 2024 behavioral-outcomes study.

Finding at a glance

Children who took part improved in self-control and cooperation; both scores were higher than in the comparison group after the program.
