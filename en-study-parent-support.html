Does social support for parents matter for adolescents?

What we found

When parents felt more supported, adolescents tended to have fewer emotional and behavioral problems and more helpful, considerate behavior.

How the study was conducted

Cross-sectional analysis of 536 parents of eighth graders.

Why it matters

Supporting parents and creating accessible networks may be valuable for family health.

How to interpret the findings
The associations do not establish direction of causation.

Finding at a glance

When parents felt more supported, adolescents tended to have fewer emotional and behavioral problems and more helpful, considerate behavior.
