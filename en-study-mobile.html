How is time spent on smartphones and tablets related to children's behavior?

What we found

Children using mobile devices for at least 60 minutes a day were more likely to show conduct problems and hyperactivity or inattention than those using them for less time.

How the study was conducted

Cross-sectional parent survey of 1,642 first graders; adjusted odds ratios 1.77 and 1.82, respectively.

Why it matters

Device use can be considered alongside routines and support at home and school.

How to interpret the findings
Sixty minutes was a study cutoff, not a proven safety threshold; causation is unknown.

Key results

Conduct problems · adjusted odds ratio1.77 (95% CI 1.03–3.04)
Hyperactivity/inattention · adjusted odds ratio1.82 (95% CI 1.15–2.87)
