How is regional variation in senility mortality associated with place of death?

What we found

Areas with a higher proportion of deaths at home or in long-term care facilities had higher standardized mortality ratios for senility; areas with a higher proportion of deaths in medical institutions had lower ratios, even after adjustment for regional characteristics.

How the study was conducted

National ecological analysis of 335 secondary medical areas, using 2020–2024 mortality data.

Why it matters

The findings can inform regional end-of-life care planning and interpretation of death statistics.

How to interpret the findings
Regional associations do not establish individual causation.
