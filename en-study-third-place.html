Does a place outside home and school support adolescent mental health?

What we found

Adolescents without a third place were more likely to have peer relationship problems. The adjusted odds ratio was 2.07; the adjusted association with emotional symptoms was not significant.

How the study was conducted

Cross-sectional survey of parents of 540 eighth graders, with propensity-score weighting.

Why it matters

Safe and accessible places for young people deserve attention in community planning.

How to interpret the findings
Odds are not probabilities, and the study cannot establish causation.

Key results

Peer problems · adjusted odds ratio2.07 (95% CI 1.01–4.23)
Emotional symptomsNot significant after adjustment
