How can parents support children's physical activity?

What we found

Children whose parents provided practical sports support tended to be more active at higher intensities. Those whose parents used community resources tended to walk and engage in moderate activity more.

How the study was conducted

Cross-sectional survey of 717 third-grade children and caregivers.

Why it matters

Practical access to activities and local facilities may matter for children's movement.

How to interpret the findings
Reported associations do not establish cause and effect.

Finding at a glance

Children whose parents provided practical sports support tended to be more active at higher intensities. Those whose parents used community resources tended to walk and engage in moderate activity more.
