How does family income relate to children's mental health?

What we found

Children from lower-income households tended to have more emotional and behavioral problems after entering elementary school.

How the study was conducted

One-year follow-up of 1,712 children from age five to six, with parent-reported family background and behavior.

Why it matters

Reducing economic barriers and supporting families may help address child health inequalities.

How to interpret the findings
Income and education do not determine an individual child's development; causation is not established.

Finding at a glance

Children from lower-income households tended to have more emotional and behavioral problems after entering elementary school.
