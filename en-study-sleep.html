Does family socioeconomic status relate to adolescents' sleep?

What we found

Adolescents from households with lower incomes or lower maternal educational attainment tended to have poorer sleep quality.

How the study was conducted

Cross-sectional questionnaire study of first-year junior high school students, analyzing socioeconomic indicators and sleep quality.

Why it matters

Sleep support should consider the family's living circumstances, not just individual habits.

How to interpret the findings
Observational self-reported data cannot establish causal effects.

Finding at a glance

Adolescents from households with lower incomes or lower maternal educational attainment tended to have poorer sleep quality.
