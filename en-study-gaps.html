Which causes of death are most closely linked to regional healthy life expectancy?

What we found

Among the major causes examined, cancer mortality had the strongest inverse association with healthy life expectancy. The regional range was 4.46 years for men and 3.46 for women.

How the study was conducted

Healthy life expectancy in 2017 and cause-specific standardized mortality in 2016–2018, analyzed by medical area.

Why it matters

Cancer prevention and smoking cessation are important considerations for local health plans.

How to interpret the findings
Ecological associations cannot quantify the causal effect of cancer prevention.

Key results

Regional range, men4.46 years
Regional range, women3.46 years
