Do walkable neighborhoods support longer healthy lives?

What we found

Older adults who reported nearby parks or sidewalks suitable for exercise had about 1.2 more years of healthy life expectancy than those who did not.

How the study was conducted

Six-year JAGES follow-up (2013–2019) of 8,956 older adults; multistate life-table analysis.

Why it matters

Walkable environments may be important complements to individual health promotion.

How to interpret the findings
Perceived environments were assessed; this is not a causal estimate of building parks.

Key results

Difference in estimated healthy life expectancyAbout +1.2 years
