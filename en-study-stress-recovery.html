Does parents' ability to recover from stress matter for children?

What we found

Parents who could relax and engage in new challenges tended to report more positive parenting and fewer emotional and behavioral problems in their children.

How the study was conducted

Cross-sectional path analysis of 536 parents of third-year junior high school students.

Why it matters

Support for parents' rest and recovery deserves attention alongside child-focused services.

How to interpret the findings
Statistical pathways in cross-sectional data do not demonstrate causation.

Finding at a glance

Parents who could relax and engage in new challenges tended to report more positive parenting and fewer emotional and behavioral problems in their children.
