Are economic circumstances related to coping among people living with HIV?

What we found

Lower income and job-seeking status were associated with weaker sense of coherence.

How the study was conducted

Regression analysis of 1,422 responses from two HIV Futures Japan surveys.

Why it matters

Economic and employment support may be relevant to psychosocial care.

How to interpret the findings
Cross-sectional associations cannot establish causality.

Key finding

Lower income and job-seeking status were associated with weaker sense of coherence.

Full text publicly accessible. Illustration is conceptual, not study data.

Original article / source：Togari T, Inoue Y, Oshima G, Abe S, Hosokawa R, Takaku Y. Socioeconomic Status and the Sense of Coherence among Japanese People Living with HIV. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. 2022; 19(13):7673.
