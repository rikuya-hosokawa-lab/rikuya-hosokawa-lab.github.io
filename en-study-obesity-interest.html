Does interest in health relate to obesity among employees?

What we found

Employees with higher health consciousness, health values and motivation tended to have lower odds of obesity.

How the study was conducted

Cross-sectional study of 2,260 IT-related employees using health checkups and questionnaires.

Why it matters

Health promotion may benefit from tailoring support to different attitudes toward health.

How to interpret the findings
The study cannot show that increasing health interest reduces obesity.

Key results

Health consciousness · adjusted odds ratio0.84
Health value · adjusted odds ratio0.85
Health motivation · adjusted odds ratio0.91
