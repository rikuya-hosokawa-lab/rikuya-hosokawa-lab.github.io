Can search trends help track population-level suicide trends?

What we found

A model using search trends reflecting everyday difficulties showed potential to capture changes in monthly suicide counts in Japan.

How the study was conducted

Time-series analysis of monthly national statistics and search trends, 2016–2020.

Why it matters

Population-level surveillance may inform timely prevention.

How to interpret the findings
Not an individual prediction tool; further validation is needed.

Key finding

A model using search trends reflecting everyday difficulties showed potential to capture changes in monthly suicide counts in Japan.
