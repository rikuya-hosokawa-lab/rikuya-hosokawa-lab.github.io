Can people living with HIV access general healthcare when needed?

What we found

About 60% of respondents had no regular general healthcare provider; more than half of that group reported needing one but not having secured one.

How the study was conducted

HIV Futures Japan online survey of 913 respondents in 2013–2014.

Why it matters

Reducing stigma and improving access to general healthcare are important public health goals.

How to interpret the findings
These findings describe a historical online survey, not today's national rates.

Finding at a glance
