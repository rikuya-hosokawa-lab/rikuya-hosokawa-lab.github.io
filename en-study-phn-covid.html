How did public health nurse staffing change during COVID-19?

What we found

Full-time staffing did not increase significantly, while the ratio of part-time to full-time nurses rose.

How the study was conducted

Survey of local governments on staffing from 2017 to 2021; 81 of 150 responded.

Why it matters

Health crisis preparedness requires sustainable workforce planning.

How to interpret the findings
Response bias and differing workloads limit interpretation.

Key finding

Full-time staffing did not increase significantly, while the ratio of part-time to full-time nurses rose.

Full text publicly accessible. Illustration is conceptual, not study data.

Original article / source：Taira K, Hosokawa R, Shiomi M. Changes in the Number of Public Health Nurses Employed in Local Governments in Japan During the Covid-19 Pandemic: A Cross-Sectional Study. SAGE Open Nursing. 2022; 8:23779608221140718.
