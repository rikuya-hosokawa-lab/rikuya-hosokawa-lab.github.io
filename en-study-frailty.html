What happens when health challenges accumulate in later life?

What we found

Older adults with risks in three or more domains had about 4.5 fewer healthy years for men and 5.7 fewer for women than those with no identified risks.

How the study was conducted

JAGES follow-up of 8,956 older adults; seven domains of the Kihon Checklist and life-table estimates at age 65.

Why it matters

Physical, psychological and social needs deserve comprehensive support.

How to interpret the findings
Group estimates do not predict an individual's lifespan or the effect of removing risks.

Key results

Men · ≥3 versus no risk domains4.46 fewer years
Women · ≥3 versus no risk domains5.68 fewer years
