What is related to self-rated health in middle and later life?

What we found

Need for help with daily living showed the strongest association with poorer self-rated health; employment and regular exercise were associated with better ratings.

How the study was conducted

Sex-stratified cross-sectional analysis of 2,826 adults aged 50 or over.

Why it matters

Health support should consider function, employment and lifestyle.

How to interpret the findings
Cross-sectional associations cannot establish causality.

Key finding

Need for help with daily living showed the strongest association with poorer self-rated health; employment and regular exercise were associated with better ratings.
