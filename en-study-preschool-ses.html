How is household income related to preschool adjustment?

What we found

Lower family income was associated with more emotional and behavioral difficulties and lower social competence.

How the study was conducted

Multivariable analyses of parental and teacher reports for 3,218 children.

Why it matters

Income and parental education should be considered separately in support policies.

How to interpret the findings
Observational study; summary based on the public abstract.

Key finding

Lower family income was associated with more emotional and behavioral difficulties and lower social competence.

Summary based on publicly available abstract. Illustration is conceptual, not study data.

Original article / source：Hosokawa R, Katsura T. Socioeconomic status, Emotional/Behavioral difficulties, and social competence among preschool children in Japan. Journal of Child and Family Studies. 2018; 27(12):4001-4014.
