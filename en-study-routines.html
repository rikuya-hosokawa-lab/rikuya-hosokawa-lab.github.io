Do daily family routines relate to children's mental health?

What we found

More consistent routines tended to go with stronger family relationships and fewer emotional and behavioral problems in children.

How the study was conducted

Path analysis of 717 valid parent responses for third graders in Japan.

Why it matters

Predictable routines and supportive relationships may be useful considerations for family support.

How to interpret the findings
Cross-sectional pathways do not establish causation.

Finding at a glance

More consistent routines tended to go with stronger family relationships and fewer emotional and behavioral problems in children.
