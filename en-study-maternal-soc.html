Is mothers’ stress coping capacity related to preschool social skills?

What we found

Higher maternal sense of coherence was associated with better child cooperation, self-control and assertion through parenting practices.

How the study was conducted

Path analysis of 1,341 maternal reports and teacher assessments.

Why it matters

Supporting parents’ well-being is relevant to child development.

How to interpret the findings
Cross-sectional evidence, not a trial of support.

Key finding

Higher maternal sense of coherence was associated with better child cooperation, self-control and assertion through parenting practices.

Original article / source：Hosokawa R, Katsura T, Shizawa M. Relations of mother's sense of coherence and childrearing style with child's social skills in preschoolers. Child and Adolescent Psychiatry and Mental Health. 2017; 11(1):11.
