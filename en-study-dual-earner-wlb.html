How is work–life balance in dual-earner families related to children’s social adjustment?

What we found

More negative work-to-family spillover was associated with higher shyness and social disinterest; positive spillover with less shyness.

How the study was conducted

Regression analysis of 443 fifth graders from dual-earner families.

Why it matters

Workplace and family support can be considered together.

How to interpret the findings
Cross-sectional associations do not establish causality.

Key finding

More negative work-to-family spillover was associated with higher shyness and social disinterest; positive spillover with less shyness.
