How do parents understand discipline and abuse?

What we found

Mothers expressed diverse views shaped by relationships and awareness of children’s rights; listening and speaking in one’s own words were important for dialogue.

How the study was conducted

Qualitative analysis of group discussions at a parenting support center.

Why it matters

Supports the design of spaces where caregivers can speak safely.

How to interpret the findings
Local qualitative findings are not population estimates.

Key finding

Mothers expressed diverse views shaped by relationships and awareness of children’s rights; listening and speaking in one’s own words were important for dialogue.
