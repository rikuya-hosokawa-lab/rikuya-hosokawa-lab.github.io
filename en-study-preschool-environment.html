2016 ｜ 小児保健研究 · Summary based on publicly available abstract

What home environments are related to preschool social skills?

What we found

Positive and autonomy-supportive parenting and family support were associated with higher social skills, whereas corporal punishment and inconsistent parenting were associated with lower skills.

How the study was conducted

Regression analysis of maternal and teacher reports for 1,341 preschoolers.

Why it matters

Supportive parenting and family resources deserve attention.

How to interpret the findings
Cross-sectional study; summary based on the accessible abstract.

Key finding

Positive and autonomy-supportive parenting and family support were associated with higher social skills, whereas corporal punishment and inconsistent parenting were associated with lower skills.
