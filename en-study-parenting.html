Does preschool discipline relate to behavior after school entry?

What we found

Overly harsh discipline was linked to more later externalizing problems in boys and girls; overly permissive discipline was linked to more problems in boys.

How the study was conducted

One-year follow-up of 1,668 children from preschool to elementary school.

Why it matters

Accessible parenting support may help families address difficulties without blame.

How to interpret the findings
Observational associations may reflect reciprocal influences.

Finding at a glance

Overly harsh discipline was linked to more later externalizing problems in boys and girls; overly permissive discipline was linked to more problems in boys.
