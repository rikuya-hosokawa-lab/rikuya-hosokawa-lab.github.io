Do regions with more healthcare resources have longer healthy lives?

What we found

Regions with more rehabilitation professionals and home healthcare resources tended to have longer healthy life expectancy, particularly among women.

How the study was conducted

Correlation analysis of 344 secondary medical areas, accounting for population density.

Why it matters

The findings can inform discussions of geographical resource allocation.

How to interpret the findings
Regional correlations do not prove that adding resources extends healthy life expectancy.

Finding at a glance

Regions with more rehabilitation professionals and home healthcare resources tended to have longer healthy life expectancy, particularly among women.
