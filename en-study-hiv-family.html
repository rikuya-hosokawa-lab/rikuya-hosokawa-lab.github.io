Are people living with HIV receiving the information they need about having children?

What we found

Only about one in five childless respondents who wanted children knew that there were ways to have children while living with HIV.

How the study was conducted

HIV Futures Japan surveys in 2013–2014 and 2016–2017 (913 and 1,038 valid responses).

Why it matters

Respecting reproductive choices requires accessible, accurate information and consultation.

How to interpret the findings
These historical online surveys do not describe current access for all people living with HIV.

Key results

Wanted information · first survey25.6%
Had received information · first survey10.0%
